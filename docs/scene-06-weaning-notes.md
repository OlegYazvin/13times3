# Frame 06: The end of breastfeeding

Oleg wants Jane and Zephrine together in a breastfeeding moment, with Jane's clothing covering her body and no exposed anatomy. The room should recognize sadness and loss at the end of a chapter while also expressing Jane's return to bodily autonomy. Keep the scene tender, domestic, and non-sexual. Do not suggest a date, medical reason, or particular difficulty that Oleg has not described.

The painting could hold the closeness of Jane and Zephrine near the front while the larger room opens into two emotional spaces: a soft, sheltering cocoon of memory and a wider space Jane can walk toward as herself. The two spaces should coexist instead of forcing a cheerful conclusion. Jane's stylized likeness can draw from her private reference photos, and Zephrine's from hers; do not publish either photograph or use 2D cutouts.

The published room has a volumetric seated Jane in a continuous sweater, Zephrine as a clothed 3D child curled across her lap, a sculpted nursing chair, a woven rug, folded blanket, blue dusk, and warm window light. Nothing below Jane's neckline is exposed. The open hand and a loosening thread express the return of agency without denying the sadness. The private photo references stay local.

The October 22 detail pass removed the rectangular sweater panel, square side table and ottoman, repeated perfect rug rings, segmented trouser legs, and separate tube arms. The rug has an uneven cloth edge and sewn threads; a turned side table holds the folded cloth.
