# Frame 05: Jane stood up for her family during the office move

Oleg says the family brought Zephrine to their workspace under an agreement, then encountered repeated objections from management. He remembers the family as friendly and the experience as unfair and difficult. The workspace was Expansive North Center. He wants an abstract treatment that conveys the strain and Jane's triumph, rather than a literal reenactment.

The [operator's North Center page](https://expansive.com/locations/illinois/chicago/chicago-north-center/) identifies the site at 3838 N Ravenswood Avenue and describes modern finishes, large windows, exposed red brick, shared work areas, and phone booths. Use those architectural cues only as a departure point. The room can bend brick and glass into constricting forms that open as Jane stands her ground; it should not name or sculpt identifiable staff, show a fabricated confrontation, quote any agreement, or present Oleg's personal account as an independently verified public claim.

Distinct from frame `02`, which celebrates Jane's sustained help during Oleg's job search. Frame `05` is about Jane's own resolve when the family faced resistance during a move.
