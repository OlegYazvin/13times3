## Real Play Garden references for this room

The [park's official Play Garden guide](https://www.maggiedaleypark.com/things-to-do-see/play-garden) describes a landscape of connected play spaces rather than a generic playground. As of 2026-10-20, it describes:

- **The Sea:** a metal play ship, blue rolling play surface, a whale, and a lighthouse tower with a slide.
- **The Harbor:** a boardwalk, central marina platform, and movable play boat for younger children.
- **The Watering Hole:** animal figures, small water features, a low wooden platform, and a shade structure for younger children.
- **Enchanted Forest:** branching tree arches, a turning stone, a circular tea-party table, and a mirrored maze.
- **Wave Lawn and Slide Crater:** rolling grass slopes, winding paths, a suspension bridge between towers, a play pyramid, and several slide forms.

The [Chicago Park District's facility page](https://www.chicagoparkdistrict.com/parks-facilities/maggie-daley-play-garden) also shows the red-and-white lighthouse-shaped slide against the city skyline.
