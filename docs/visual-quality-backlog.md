# Visual quality audit for the published rooms

This is a working art review of the eleven published worlds, based on desktop and portrait-phone walkthroughs on 2026-10-22. It does not reserve new memories or change the [scene registry](scene-registry.md). The target remains a coherent, richly detailed 3D storybook experience in **every** room. The `f28cbb7` iteration replaces the portrait masks with dimensional sculptures. The current rooms are functional, but figures still have stiff shoulders and generic expressions, and several environments remain schematic.

Oleg appears in `02`, `04`, `05`, `07`, `08`, `09`, `10`, and `11`. His current shared head uses modeled facial structure, glasses, hair and beard, but identity and figure finish remain an open art review in **all eight** rooms. Compare both private reference photos against normal doorway, close front, and three-quarter views before calling that likeness complete. All outdoor rooms (`02`, `03`, `04`, `07`, `08`, `09`, `10`) retain independent tall sky worlds; they can overlap in model coordinates behind neighboring paintings because the renderer stencils one active world at a time.
