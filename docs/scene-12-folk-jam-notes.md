## Revision observations

The first export was too empty and placed the children’s attention incorrectly.
The gathering was tightened, their gaze corrected, the window reveals rebuilt,
and the two venue interiors composed with a continuous timber floor, woven rug,
joined ceiling, compact bars, cafe furniture and warm pools of light. The rear
musicians were turned toward their own circle. The current cast has 13 people.

Actual surface inspection found both guitars passing into the seated thighs.
Their complete instrument and arm arrangements were raised by 116.1 mm. The
new sampled clearance is about 6.6 mm, with no sampled thigh penetration. The
listening hands and chair tops were fitted to the actual posed trousers; selected
lap contacts are within 0.4 mm without sampled penetration. The fiddle bow was
fitted to its curled hand, approximately 0.3 mm away. These are narrow sampled
checks, not proof that every surface in the scene is intersection-free.

Strumming and plucking fingertips are frozen between strokes, about 2–9 mm from
the actual sounding surfaces. Their free space is intentional; neck support,
resting hands and instrument/body clearance are assessed separately. The
anatomy is stylized and does not establish professional instrument technique.

The current download is about 8.56 MB including the painting, slightly above the
approximate 8 MB loading target. The larger cast and essential instrument forms
were preserved. Desktop and fresh emulated portrait-phone loads passed; physical iPhone frame rate is not claimed.


A later view found the seated adults too compressed vertically. Their upper-body
heights now preserve the standing cast’s shoulder-to-hip proportions. The child
friend’s lower hips and continuous clothed seated support meet the rug, while
her taller seated torso keeps a plausible child silhouette. The broad room is an
interpretation of the two places together, not a dimensionally exact venue survey.
The ceiling and window-street layer remain restrained; fine playing technique
and supporting-character specificity can receive further artistic polish.


The final environment pass closes both arched end walls to the actual vault,
mounts the chandeliers and mirror ball to that surface, and completes the
upstairs stools’ supports. Zephrine’s cream rosette-patterned blouse follows
the supplied jam photos; it is an original painted material, not photo pixels.
